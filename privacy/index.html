Privacy Policy for dither

Last updated: 14 August 2026

dither is a task management app. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it — in short: it stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

What dither stores, and where

All the content you create in dither — to-dos, notes, pages, tags, and your app settings — is stored locally on your device in a private database. We do not operate any server that receives or stores this data, and we do not have access to it.

Sync

If you choose to enable sync, you point dither at a folder on a storage service you already control (for example Google Drive, Dropbox, or a local folder shared by Syncthing). dither writes its data into that folder using your device's normal file-access permissions. That data is then subject to whichever service you chose to store it in — we do not run the sync service ourselves and never see this data pass through anything we operate.

Purchases

dither offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full app after a trial period. On Android, iOS and Mac, purchases are handled entirely by that platform's own billing system (Google Play or the App Store); we do not collect or store your payment details, and we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, used solely to unlock the app on your device. On Windows, purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record for that checkout — dither itself never sees your payment details, and the app unlocks via a license key that is verified entirely offline, on your device.

Notifications

Reminders you set in dither are scheduled locally on your device using your operating system's notification system. No reminder content or scheduling data is sent to us or to any third party.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

dither does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not collect usage data, device identifiers, or any other information about how you use the app.

Data sharing

Because we do not collect any data from you, there is nothing for us to share with third parties.

Children's privacy

dither is not directed at children and is not designed to collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If dither's data practices change in the future, this page will be updated to reflect that, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to squeakybadger@gmail.com.
