Where Things 3 still has the edge

Credit where it's due — Things 3 has had a decade to sand down every rough edge, and it shows.

Maturity

Things 3 has been refined since 2017. dither is newer — expect it to keep closing small polish gaps over time.

Native Apple integration

Things 3 goes deeper on Apple-only surfaces built up over years: Shortcuts actions, Apple Watch, a longer-established Mail-to-Things flow.

Ecosystem familiarity

If you've used Things 3 for years and never touch a non-Apple device, cross-platform reach may simply not matter to you — and that's a fair reason to stay.
